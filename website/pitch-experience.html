01 — The Pitches

What each brief was really asking

Every card is set out the same way: the brief, the idea the proposal put forward, the section I owned, and why that idea holds up. "Overall creative planning" was team work on all six; the section under "What I owned" was mine alone.

01
夸克 Quark
Search · consumer app
2024
The brief

Win a crowded gaokao-season pitch — the same audience, in the same three weeks, with the same promise as every other search and education app.

The idea

Sell a free volunteer-application advisory service, not a search box — a "专业军师团" of three named strategists instead of one interchangeable spokesperson.

Two poster systems carried it: a four-step feature guide rebuilt per persona, and a pain-point series where the answer to every question is a screenshot of the product itself.

报志愿怕「浪费分数」怎么办 → 【模拟选志愿】 选大学 A，还是选专业 B？→ 不知选啥就选 Q
What I owned
产品功能传播 — 产品功能向创意物料示意 pp. 54–60 of the proposal

The product-feature leg of the deck: the four-step feature guide built per persona (province, subject combination, score and worry all different for each) and the pain-point poster series that pairs every worry with the product screen that removes it.

I also proposed the "专业军师" concept, which was adopted into the proposal and became its slogan: 专业军师，轻松选择.

Why it holds up

It answers the question a parent is actually asking — what happens if we waste this score? — instead of listing features. And the poster system is a template, not six separate designs: the same layout regenerates for any province and subject combination, which is what makes it producible inside a gaokao window.

02
小度 Xiaodu (Baidu)
AI hardware · education tech
2024
The brief

Differentiate an AI learning tablet that was losing a pure feature comparison on Douyin. Every competitor was shouting the same benefit at the same parent: 省妈 — "it saves the mother".

The idea

Move the product from a tool that frees up a parent to a role inside the family — 家有AI更有爱. The machine sits between parent and child instead of replacing one of them.

What I owned
平台资源与 IP 热点 — the Douyin in-platform play pp. 35–47 of the proposal

The Douyin in-platform IP tie-in and the campaign's flagship challenge. I designed #60分贝有爱辅导挑战: the insight is that a parent's frustration during homework has one signature tell — they raise their voice. So the mechanic is a Douyin AR effect with a decibel meter built into it, and the challenge is to keep your voice under 60dB while tutoring.

Rollout runs as a creator relay: a head KOL opens the topic, three to five mid-tier KOLs carry it, then each @-mentions the next so the fan pools interconnect. The content beat is fixed in advance — first attempt alone fails, second attempt with the tablet succeeds.

抖音特效置入分贝测试组件 达人接力三步走 失败 → 转折 → 种草
Why it holds up

It turns an invisible problem into a measurable, filmable one. "I lose my temper over homework" is not a post; a decibel reading that spikes into the red is — and the failure is what makes the content, so every creator has something worth posting.

03
起点读书 × 庆余年2
Reading app · IP partnership
2024
The brief

Raise the brand's association with a flagship IP beyond the standard episode tie-in — where every platform buys the same star and posts the same stills.

The idea

The proposal's offline answer was a 上脑招式 series — three escalating moves. The flagship was a book-to-place travel collaboration with 携程: two routes, 寻找起源 (the places the book came from) and 沉浸观景 (the places it was filmed).

Around that sat a set of city-level activations — the part of the deck that had to work on a street, not on a slide.

What I owned
起点剧上脑的有声书 — the audiobook activations Within 线下创意部分 · pp. 44–68 of the proposal

Two offline events, both built to put a reading app into a public space with no screen anywhere in it.

剧上脑的移动书摊 — the insight is that a street stall is the sound of a city: “锵剪子、磨菜刀……”, “油炸糕……”. Adults no longer have the patience for print, but they will listen. So a 起点红 audiobook stall is wheeled through the busy streets of 天津 · 上海 · 广州, staffed by a 说书大爷 in traditional 长衫, dark glasses and a folding fan, with a 五竹 bodyguard in costume standing behind him. The stall carries 起点 IP cover posters, a QR code, a speaker, 糖葫芦, a teapot and a chicken leg. Douyin's silver-haired creators — @王德顺, @末那大叔, @康康和爷爷 — were booked as the storytellers.

起点十三城剧上脑的伴手礼 — tied to 范闲大婚 in the drama: a deliberately light giveaway built into thirteen subway stations across thirteen cities — 上海塘桥 · 深圳民治 · 成都春熙路 · 重庆沙坪坝 · 杭州龙翔桥 · 武汉宝通寺 · 西安小寨 · 天津营口道 · 苏州东方之门 · 南京东方福来德 · 郑州二七广场 · 长沙五一广场 · 青岛台东. Wedding sweets and gifts handed out at rush hour, with a check-in wall where passers-by wrote a line about the book or the show.

#大爷用说书的方式打开网络爽文# #范闲林婉儿在地铁站发喜糖# #下班路上看见范闲在地铁站发喜糖#
Why it holds up

The book stall is a sound format for a product about listening — you hear the 吆喝 before you see the stall, which is exactly how an audiobook works. And the contrast is the whole idea: a web-novel brand that has been online since 2002 choosing a street stall and an old man as its delivery mechanism.

The subway giveaway goes the opposite way and stays deliberately light. A rush-hour crowd will not stop, so it is built to be understood in five seconds with one hand — and by hanging it on the drama's biggest emotional beat, the gift arrived with its own reason to be accepted.

04
饿了么 × 巴黎奥运
Food delivery · event tie-in
2024
The brief

Turn Olympic attention into app engagement — during a fortnight when every brand in the country was buying the same association, and a food-delivery app has no rights to any of it.

The idea

Run an in-app 夏季运动会 — a series of mini-games released in sync with the Olympic schedule, each one matching a different sport, with free-delivery draws as the reward. Four games, four events:

射击 → 《百发百中》 游泳 → 《泳气大爆发》 霹雳舞 → 《酷炫霹雳》 乒乓球 → 《有球必应》
What I owned
【玩法介绍】游戏部分 — the game mechanic The gameplay section of the proposal

The rule set that turns four mini-games into one campaign mechanic:

① Play the games, accumulate points and 吃货豆; crossing a points threshold unlocks a free-delivery draw — capped at one per player per day, so the campaign lasts the fortnight rather than one afternoon.

② Draw winners are then ranked by province. If a player's province places top three, they get an extra draw at a ¥10 red packet.

③ The blue rider appears as a support character, handing out scoring tips — written as the "strongest assist", modelled on the way a game like 王者荣耀 uses 妲己.

Why it holds up

The daily cap is what makes it a campaign instead of a giveaway, and the province ranking is what makes it social — a player is no longer playing for a voucher, they are playing for their province. And the rider only appears to help, which is the cheapest possible way to make a delivery mascot mean something.

05
UC 浏览器 × 功夫熊猫4
Browser · film IP
2024
The brief

Tie a browser's entertainment features to a film IP — without the whole thing reading as an advert for the film.

The idea

Use Po's signature moves as the demonstration format: each martial-arts move performs a different UC feature, so the film supplies the visual language and the browser supplies the payload. The activity line is built on an H5 quiz users play and share.

What I owned
活动线-创意内容 → 1、题库内容建议 The first item of the activity-line content plan

The question-bank design for the H5 quiz — four question types, each chosen for a reason and each wired to a different UC feature:

打破惯性思维 — 海龟汤 and lateral-thinking puzzles. Odd, witty, deliberately hard; the tie-in is "can't work it out? search it on UC."

冷知识 — facts built to stand alone as a hot-search entry, in the shape of the ones Weibo already carries (e.g. #贡菜就是晒干的莴笋#).

网络热梗 — borrows the traffic of a meme that is already moving.

超纲类 — wuxia and film trivia, niche enough to start an argument, and the closest fit to the Panda IP itself.

The bank then became the brief for the KOL question-setters the pitch proposed — a legal question from 罗翔, a memory question from 闲人王昱珩 — so the quiz had a reason to be different every day.

Why it holds up

A quiz is the cheapest possible content format and the easiest to make boring. Sorting the bank by why the player would want the answer — curiosity, trivia, a meme they already know, an argument they want to win — is what makes people play more than one round, and each type hands UC a different feature to be useful with.

06
只用淘宝
E-commerce · UGC activation
2023
The brief

Reinforce "you can get anything on Taobao" — with no new feature to launch and nothing to announce.

The idea

「只用淘宝实现一万个可能挑战杯」 — a public UGC challenge presented as an awards ceremony for ordinary users, not a marketing campaign. A four-week calendar of themed challenges, each capped at 200元, closing with an awards show whose categories are jokes about what users actually did.

第一周 年底养生计划 第二周 年底旅行新姿势 第三周 仪式感跨年挑战 第四周 新年新角色挑战
What I owned
年底养生计划 — week one of the challenge calendar pp. 27–30 of the proposal

The opening week, built on the observation that this generation's wellness is technical rather than disciplined: 质疑养生 → 理解养生 → 热爱养生. Three 200元 challenges, each wired to a different Taobao service:

脆皮大学生在宿舍开养生馆 — buy the equipment (艾草按摩捶, 刮痧板) on a 200元 budget and open a mini wellness studio in a dorm room, using 省钱助手 to cut the cost and 情绪树洞 for the emotional half of 养生.

20岁提前体验一周退休人生 — plan a week of retirement living on 200元, covering as many daily scenarios as Taobao can serve.

00后打造一周养生餐不重样 — seven days of wellness meals on 200元, no repeats, with 生鲜 for ingredients and 薄荷健康 for the calorie maths behind the menu.

Why it holds up

Wellness is usually sold as discipline — buy this, do this. Framing it as something you can do cheaply and technically makes it a challenge rather than a purchase, and the 200元 cap means the brand is the constraint, not the sponsor. That is the only way a UGC challenge can actually demonstrate a claim like "you can get anything here".

02 — Selected Pitch Artwork

The answer is the product

Four posters from my section of the Quark gaokao proposal — 产品功能向创意物料示意, pp. 54–60. The pain-point series is built as question-and-answer pairs: a worry in plain language, and a companion poster where the answer is a screenshot of the feature itself. The choice format flattens a question nobody can answer into a binary the product can settle.

Pain point · 1 of 6

报志愿 怕「浪费分数」怎么办

The answer

冲稳保一键get → 【模拟选志愿】你的分数管理大师

The choice format

选大学 A，还是选专业 B？不知选啥就选 Q

Feature guide · templated

河北 · 史地政 · 620分 — one of a set rebuilt for each persona

Why the pairing matters. A pain-point poster on its own just repeats the customer's anxiety back at them. Pairing it with an answer poster — and making the answer a screenshot of the actual interface — turns two posters into one argument: here is the worry, here is the screen that removes it. That structure is what makes the set scale: swap the question, swap the screenshot. It is also why the feature guide is built per persona rather than as one generic walkthrough — the province, the subject combination and the score are all variables in the same template.
Why this page is here

The half of the job a case study can't show

Every one of the six proposals had a section that was mine to carry, and each card names it — 产品功能创意物料, 抖音站内 IP 与挑战机制, 剧上脑的有声书, 游戏玩法规则, 题库内容, 一周挑战设计 — together with the page range it sits on.

Naming the section and the page is how I separate what I carried from what the team carried — and on every card, the part that was team work is labelled as team work.

What the six show is the part of the job that leaves no other trace — reading a brief with no obvious answer, finding the pressure the audience is actually under, and building a mechanic rather than a slogan.
