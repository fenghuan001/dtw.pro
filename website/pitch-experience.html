01
Quark
Search · consumer app
2024
The brief

Communicate Quark’s university and major-selection tools during the gaokao application season.

My contribution

Developed product-feature mockups, including persona-based guides and pain-point copy. Proposed “专业军师” (an expert guide to choosing a major), which was incorporated into the team’s proposal.

Selected concept

专业军师，轻松选择

Pair a student’s application concern with the relevant product screen, then demonstrate the selection process using different scores, provinces and subject combinations.

02
Xiaodu (Baidu)
AI hardware · education tech
2024
The brief

Develop a distinctive Douyin campaign for Xiaodu’s AI learning tablet.

My contribution

Developed the Douyin platform-IP approach and a creator challenge linking homework frustration with the tablet’s proposed role in family learning.

Selected concept

#60分贝有爱辅导挑战

Proposed an AR decibel-meter effect inviting parents to keep their voices below 60dB while tutoring. A planned KOL relay would show a difficult homework moment followed by a tablet-assisted attempt.

Qidian Reading × Joy of Life 2
Reading app · IP partnership
2024
The brief

Connect Qidian Reading with Joy of Life 2 through offline experiences.

My contribution

Developed two audiobook-related offline activation concepts within the team’s broader IP proposal.

01

移动有声书摊

A mobile storytelling stall would bring audiobook excerpts into busy public spaces, with themed performers and a QR code linking back to Qidian.

02

剧上脑的伴手礼

A proposed subway giveaway would use Fan Xian’s wedding storyline to connect themed sweets, gifts and a check-in wall with the reading app.

04
Ele.me · Summer Sports
Food delivery · event tie-in
2024
The brief

Create a summer sports-themed activation that connects entertainment with Ele.me app participation.

My contribution

Developed gameplay rules, reward limits, province rankings and the delivery-rider support role for the proposed mini-game campaign.

Selected concept

夏季运动会

Four sports-themed games would award points and food-reward tokens (“吃货豆”), unlocking one delivery-reward draw per player per day. Top-ranked provinces would receive an additional ¥10 red-packet draw; a rider character would provide gameplay tips.

Taobao · Everyday Challenges
E-commerce · UGC activation
2023
The brief

Demonstrate the breadth of Taobao’s products and services through everyday challenges.
