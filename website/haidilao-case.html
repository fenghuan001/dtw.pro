01 — The Brief

What the campaign had to do

Background only. Everything from "My Contribution" onwards is my own output.

Yihai International launched three new self-heating hot pot flavours for 618, built around one product story: "Early Hu, Late Hong" — Pepper Pork Tripe Chicken by day, Hot & Sour Tomato Beef by night.

The campaign ran across Weibo, Xiaohongshu and Douyin, a street pop-up in Qingdao, a ticketed music party, a co-branding partnership with Line Dog, and traditional media PR.

Commercial goalDrive a sales surge during 618 and bring in new customers.
Brand goalCreate an offline talking point, grow topic search, and reach 200–300M impressions.
Audience
Students 18–24 (primary) · young white-collar 25–30 · over 60% female
Product
Three new flavours · RMB 29.9 per box
Timing
Teaser 15–31 May · Burst 1–18 June · Extension 18–20 June
Lead idea
"一刻钟" — a quarter of an hour as the unit of comfort
02 — My Contribution

Six workstreams, five owned end to end

Solo

Official publishing plan

Wrote the full 618 publishing schedule and copy for Weibo and Xiaohongshu — including the "lost chef hat" teaser that opened the campaign.

Solo

Co-branding activation

Wrote the concept and the comment-section copy for the "find an Early-Hu-Late-Hong meal buddy" activation with partner brands.

Solo

Traditional media PR

Wrote the PR brief and liaised directly with media to place the article, keeping every mention attributed to Yihai as the client required.

Solo

Offline event design

Designed the Big Day zone layout — the street pop-up areas (tasting, photo, lottery, costume, shuttle) and the Livehouse area.

Solo

KOL sourcing & management

Built and managed a list of around 60 local Qingdao food KOLs, tracking follower tier, venue, publish date and per-video performance.

Contributed

Douyin strategy & poster copy

Wrote the Douyin content strategy section of the master deck, and the copy for the text-only poster series.
