01 — Background

Yihai International’s 618 campaign promoted new self-heating hot pot flavours through social content, a Line Dog collaboration and offline events in Qingdao. The creative theme connected everyday moments of comfort with a morning-and-evening dining routine.

02 — My Contribution

My role across content and campaign delivery

Independent deliverable

Campaign Proposal — Poster Copy & Mockups

Independently developed poster copy and mockups for the integrated campaign proposal, translating product benefits into everyday situations and moments of comfort.

Independent deliverable

Douyin Communication Strategy

Independently wrote the Douyin communication strategy section of the integrated campaign proposal.

Independent deliverable · On-site execution

Offline Event Planning & Execution

Developed the event-zone plan for the Qingdao activation. On site, photographed customers purchasing self-heating hot pots, assisted with product sales, and guided KOL filming to support campaign content creation.

Team contribution · Independent deliverable

Local Influencer Coordination & Video Outline

Coordinated content delivery with 38 local influencers, reviewing submissions and following up on deadlines. Independently wrote a video outline for Douyin creator Xie Xiaoyang (解小羊), with the first proposed direction adopted.

The media team handled creator selection, invitations, bookings and performance tracking.

Independent deliverable

Social Publishing Plan & Copy

Independently developed the Weibo and Xiaohongshu publishing schedule and campaign copy, including the “lost chef hat” teaser, for the brand team to publish.

Independent deliverable · Team contribution

PR Briefing & Media Coordination

Independently wrote the PR brief and coordinated with the media team to support article placement.

05 — Selected Work / Poster Copy

Poster copy for everyday moments

Six copy concepts I independently wrote, connecting everyday pressures with a fifteen-minute food break and extending the theme to a live music activation. Brief English notes explain the Chinese wordplay.

#肚鸡一刻钟 喝汤好轻松
一刻钟很长， 长到会议一秒都听不下去。 一刻钟很短， 短到胡椒猪肚鸡汤等不及就下肚了。
只用一刻钟，轻松到胃
01 · A contrast in time

A meeting makes fifteen minutes feel long; anticipation of hot soup makes it feel short.

#肚鸡一刻钟 喝汤好轻松
长大后发现， 做什么事情都不轻松 :( 但这碗胡椒猪肚鸡汤， 让我感到久违的轻松 :)
饮汤轻松，暖胃又暖心
02 · Everyday reassurance

The copy contrasts the difficulty of adult life with a small, accessible moment of comfort.

#肚鸡一刻钟 喝汤好轻松
试过了公园二十分钟理论， 那这次可以试试 胡椒猪肚鸡汤十五分钟理论。
用一刻钟治愈一下
03 · A familiar wellbeing reference

The “twenty-minute park” reference is adapted into a fifteen-minute soup break.

#肚鸡一刻钟 喝汤好轻松
你总说白天不懂夜的黑， 但红酸汤能用一刻钟 读懂你的夜。
早胡晚红，好轻松
04 · The evening product story

A familiar lyric about day and night introduces red soup as a source of evening comfort.

#肚鸡一刻钟 喝汤好轻松
一天中有辛酸、腰酸， 还有氨基酸。 经过这么多种酸， 就让美味红酸汤用一刻钟的时间 以"酸"攻酸。
忙碌过后来一碗
05 · Wordplay around 酸

The shared character 酸 connects hardship, physical soreness and amino acids with the soup’s sour flavour.
