01 — The Brief

What the campaign had to do

Background only. Everything from "My Contribution" onwards is my own output.

Quark Scan King is a document-processing app for scanning, OCR and file conversion. In its early growth phase it needed to stand out in a crowded scanning-tool category, build name recognition, and give users a reason to remember it.

The campaign was built around the app's December version release, with "intelligence" as the unifying product claim — and was run as two deliberately different lines off one brief.

AwarenessBreak out of a category where every tool makes similar functional claims — and create a memory hook rather than a feature list.
DifferentiationFrame the release as "a new kind of scanning", not just an update to an existing app.
Product
Scanning · OCR · file conversion
Hook
December version release
Category
Crowded, with near-identical functional claims
Approach
Two parallel lines, one budget, one review
Line 01

Product line — awareness

Challenge the questions people actually have about old scanning tools, then answer them in a single line: with Quark Scan King there are far fewer questions — it's just smart and easy to use.

  • 1 TVC · functional poster set · teaser posters
  • KOLs: 黄子弘凡 vlog · 差评君 video
Line 02

Dunhuang line — credibility

A cultural partnership that proved the product better than any demo. Visitors physically experienced its two hardest features — high-definition scanning and line-art extraction — then took the output home and used it.

  • Guomao subway mural exhibition, 25–29 December
  • In-app Dunhuang gallery and re-colouring challenge
  • KOLs: illustrators, photographers, cosplayers, cultural voices

05 — Selected Work / Dunhuang KOC Materials

Proving a feature with a cultural partnership

Materials produced across five functional angles — exhibition, line-art extraction, HD scanning, remix and subway. Three of them below.

Exhibition angle
「我为敦煌上新色」

The in-app exhibition page — mural, story panel, and the single action the campaign wanted: extract the line art and re-colour it.

Line-art extraction
「一键提取线稿好方便！」

The product's hardest feature in one image: coloured mural in, clean line art out. This is what a demo cannot do and a physical exhibition can.

Remix angle
「我和妹妹一起画滴~」

The printed line-art card after a real visitor coloured it — closing the loop from subway poster to app activity to something on a family's table.

Why this line existed: the product line could claim intelligence, but only the Dunhuang line let people verify it. Visitors scanned a real mural, watched the line art come out clean, and took the output home to use — which is a harder proof than any claim in a poster.
06 — Results
110M+ Weibo reads at an overall CPM of 2.7.

Both lines ran off one budget and were measured in a single review, which meant the product line and the cultural partnership could be read against each other rather than separately.
