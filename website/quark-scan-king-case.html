01 — Background

The campaign supported Quark Scan King’s December version release through two streams: communicating upgraded product features and demonstrating scanning and line-art extraction through a Dunhuang cultural partnership.

Line 01

Product Awareness

Highlighted the app’s upgraded scanning and document-processing features through everyday work and study situations.

Line 02

Dunhuang Partnership

Used Dunhuang artwork to demonstrate HD scanning and line-art extraction, inviting users to explore and recolour the images.
