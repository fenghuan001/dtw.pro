01 — Background

Juhuasuan’s Northeast New Year campaign used regional food, winter scenery and humour to build interest in its 3 January livestream with Liu Wei and Jin Jing. Douyin content connected the offline campaign with invitations to watch the livestream, claim red packets and shop for local specialities.

02 — My Contribution

My contribution to the Douyin campaign

Team contribution

Topic proposal

Developed a topic linking Jin Jing’s livestream appearance with the “Southern potato” conversation around Northeast winter tourism.

Independent deliverable

Topic briefs & creator scripts

Wrote a separate brief for every Douyin topic so each seeding account had its own angle — plus three Douyin Xingtu creator scripts.

Independent deliverable

Content quality control

Reviewed 100+ Douyin creator scripts and videos against brand messaging and platform rules before publication.

Team contribution

Vendor management

Team contribution
