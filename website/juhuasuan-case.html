01 — The Brief

What the campaign had to do

Background only. Everything from "My Contribution" onwards is my own output.

Harbin's winter tourism boom made China's Northeast the biggest social conversation of the 2023–24 winter. Juhuasuan ran a New Year campaign themed 「一年又一年，没什么大不了」 — using Northeastern food, scenery and humour to pull users back into the app for red packets and local speciality purchases.

The campaign climaxed in a live stream on 3 January with 刘维 and 金靖, with all social content feeding toward it.

Break throughThe Northeast was saturated with brand content, and the "Southern potato" (南方小土豆) label had already become the dominant narrative.
ConvertTurn that attention into app visits — red packets claimed and local specialities bought.

03 — Selected Work / Topic

The topic I proposed

Trended
抖音 · 娱乐榜
Douyin Entertainment Chart
#26
#金靖说自己是南方小土豆#

金靖 was one of the two live-stream guests on 3 January. The strongest available angle was not another scenic shot of Harbin — it was the comedian naming herself as the very "Southern potato" the internet had already been laughing about. That turned a brand-owned trend into a person-owned moment.

Run as a portfolio of bets, not a single bet

Multiple topic candidates were seeded in parallel by high-reach Douyin accounts, and the platform decided which surfaced. I proposed one of them — and it trended. Several others also cleared 10M+ impressions.
